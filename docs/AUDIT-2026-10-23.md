### Why BN14 looked stalled

1. **The game was closed for about 33h of the 60.6h** (two gaps: ~22.6h and ~10.2h,
   shown by Bladeburner's `storedCycles` and the console log). Nothing can buy, install
   or run black ops offline. About 20h of Bladeburner bonus time is still stored, and it
   only plays while the game is open.
2. **The aug buyer and the stock trader deadlocked.** The buyer only spent cash; the
   trader keeps 10% of net worth as cash and invests the rest. The dearest ready aug
   (Omnibeam, $78.4b) sat just above that 10%, so the buyer saved for it forever while
   13 ready augs, including $1.6b-$23.5b ones, went unbought. BN14 installs need 4 queued,
   so no install could fire either.
3. **Server buying froze.** `maybeBuyInfra` returns "Saving for Aug" whenever the target
   is buyable, and its money-short branch could never pass its own reserve. BN14's
   botnet is entirely RAM-bound: 1TB of server costs $56m and pays back in about 80s.
4. **Operation Tyrell was gated at 90% success.** Its real chance is about 65% (the bot
   sees 61%); a failure costs about 100 rank of 70k.
5. With The Blade's Simulacrum, the free work slot sat on Homicide (karma is long past
   the gang gate) instead of working faction rep for the agility/strength augs.
